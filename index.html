MyTab Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2025

Summary: MyTab does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All your data stays locally on your device.

What is MyTab?

MyTab is a Chrome extension that replaces your new tab page with a customizable dashboard featuring bookmarks, Chrome bookmark folder sync, 30 animated backgrounds, and a search bar.

Data Collection

MyTab does NOT collect any personal information. Specifically:

• No analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind.
• No browsing history, cookies, or personal data is accessed.
• No data is ever sent to our servers (we don't have servers).
• No advertisements are displayed.
• No third-party SDKs are included.

Data Storage

Your bookmarks, groups, and settings are stored using Chrome's built-in storage API:

chrome.storage.local — Stores all your bookmarks, settings, custom cover images, and fetched favicon icons locally on your device. No data is synced to any cloud service or leaves your machine.

Network Requests

MyTab makes network requests only as a fallback to fetch website favicon (icon) images when Chrome's local cache does not have them. The following services may be used:

• Google Favicon Service: https://www.google.com/s2/favicons
• Favicon.im: https://favicon.im

These requests send only the domain name (e.g., "github.com") to retrieve the website's icon. No other information is transmitted.

Permissions Explained

storage — Required to save your bookmarks and settings locally on your device.

bookmarks — Required for the Chrome bookmark folder binding and import features. MyTab reads your bookmark tree to let you bind folders and listens for bookmark changes to keep bound groups in sync. MyTab does NOT modify, delete, or create any bookmarks in your Chrome browser.

favicon — Required to access Chrome's built-in favicon cache to display website icons on bookmark cards. This only reads locally cached icons already stored by Chrome and does not make any additional network requests.

host_permissions (google.com/s2/favicons, gstatic.com, favicon.im) — Used as a fallback to fetch website icons when Chrome's local favicon cache does not have them. Only the domain name is sent; no other data is transmitted.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any data to third parties. We do not have access to your data at all.

Children's Privacy

MyTab does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact
