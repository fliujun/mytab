MyTab Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2025

Summary: MyTab does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All your data stays locally on your device. MyTab makes no network requests whatsoever.

What is MyTab?

MyTab is a Chrome extension that replaces your new tab page with a customizable dashboard featuring bookmarks, Chrome bookmark folder sync, a calculator, a translator, 30 animated backgrounds, and a todo list.

Data Collection

MyTab does NOT collect any personal information. Specifically:

• No analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind.
• No browsing history, cookies, or personal data is accessed.
• No data is ever sent to any external server (we don't have servers).
• No advertisements are displayed.
• No third-party SDKs are included.

Data Storage

Your bookmarks, groups, settings, and widget data are stored using Chrome's built-in storage API:

chrome.storage.local — Stores all your bookmarks, settings, and custom cover images locally on your device. No data is synced to any cloud service or leaves your machine.

Network Requests

MyTab does not make any network requests. All functionality operates entirely offline using local data and browser APIs.

Permissions Explained

storage — Required to save your bookmarks, settings, and widget data locally on your device.

bookmarks — Required for the Chrome bookmark folder sync and import features. MyTab reads your bookmark tree to let you sync folders and listens for bookmark changes to keep synced groups up to date. MyTab does NOT modify, delete, or create any bookmarks in your Chrome browser.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any data to third parties. We do not have access to your data at all.

Children's Privacy

MyTab does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store developer page or open an issue on our GitHub repository.
